## 1. Project Background

For this project, I want to understand how IEEE 802.11e improves Quality of Service (QoS) in wireless LANs. Wireless networks carry different types of traffic such as normal data, voice, and video, and these do not all have the same network requirements.

For example, voice and video are more sensitive to delays than normal data traffic. This means that treating all traffic in the same way can affect the quality of multimedia applications.

My main focus will therefore be on understanding how IEEE 802.11e handles different types of traffic and gives priority to traffic that is more sensitive to delay.
